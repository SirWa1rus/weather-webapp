# Problem

People's lives revolve around the Earth's weather. Without some way of tracking it, people may not know what clothes to wear, important events/gatherings might be ruined, or people may not know that a natural disaster like a tornado or flood will affect their location.

# Initial Requirements

The program must:

    -   ask the user for a location
    -   provide the user with the hourly weather, the high and lo, if any unusual weather patterns are occuring in the area, the air quality, sunrise/sunset times, and if its cloudy, partly cloudy, sunny, etc (weather in both celsius and fahrenheit).
